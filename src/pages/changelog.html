Changelog

Changes to the spec, the examples, this site and the standards process, newest first. Spec-only changes are also listed in spec section 6.

  1. 2026-09-24

    Entitlement and payment fields, site launch, registrations

    • Spec. Optional entitlement and payment fields added to the RightsSummary and PlaybackDescriptor, entitlement_ref and payment_ref to the UsageReceipt, and entitlement_url and payment_terms_url to the Manifest. Defined in 1.0, implementation optional. Section 3.3.
    • Examples. New restricted rights summary that needs a linked account. The base examples carry the free forms.
    • Site. agentreadyvideo.org launched with a quick start, a versioned spec URL at /spec/1.0, an implementations page and this changelog.
    • IANA. Provisional registration request for the arv: URI scheme submitted.
    • W3C. Agent-Ready Video Community Group proposed. Not yet formed.
    • schema.org. Guidance issue filed on marking up video moments as Clips: schemaorg/schemaorg#4923.
  2. 2026-09-23

    Draft 1.0 proposed

    • Spec. Agent-Ready Video (ARV) Draft 1.0 proposed: moments, per-moment rights, evidence, playback descriptors, usage receipts, bindings to thirteen standards and three conformance levels.
    • Examples. Seven example objects served as JSON at /examples/1.0/.

Follow changes as they happen on GitHub.
