The spec text may be forked under its license. Use of the name "Agent-Ready Video", the short form "ARV" and the badge follows a short conformance mark policy: you may say your site conforms at a level only when a current validator report shows it.

How a feature becomes required

  1. Propose. Anyone opens an issue or pull request on GitHub that says what the feature is, who needs it, and which existing standard it reuses.
  2. Draft as optional. The change lands in the draft as MAY or SHOULD, with example objects, schema changes and validator checks in the same change. It is listed in spec section 6 and the changelog.
  3. Implement twice. Two independent implementations pass the validator for the feature. At least one is not the maintainer's.
  4. Make it required. Only then does the feature become MUST, in the next version, with a dated entry in the changelog.
  5. Freeze the version. Published schema versions and version URLs never change. A breaking change gets a new version path, such as /spec/1.1 or /schema/2.0/.

Status and roadmap

  • Now: Draft 1.0, proposed, published 2026-09-23 at /spec/1.0. Spec text, example objects, the quick start and llms.txt are live. Every change is in the changelog.
  • Next: JSON Schemas at fixed URLs, the validator and badge, and three starters.
  • Proposed: a W3C Community Group. It is not formed yet. A Community Group can publish reports, but those are not W3C Recommendations.
  • Submitted: provisional registration of the arv: URI scheme with IANA, requested 2026-09-24.
  • Filed: a schema.org guidance issue on Clip ids for video moments, schemaorg/schemaorg#4923.
  • Not claimed: no implementations yet (see implementations). The draft stays "proposed" until an outside one passes the validator.
