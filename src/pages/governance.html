Governance

ARV is maintained by AgentCDN with open contributions. The spec is a draft, and it says "proposed" until an outside implementation passes the validator.

Maintainer

AgentCDN maintains the spec, the schemas and the validator. Contact: team@agentreadyvideo.org.

The spec text may be forked under its license. Use of the name "Agent-Ready Video", the short form "ARV" and the badge follows a short conformance mark policy: you may say your site conforms at a level only when a current validator report shows it.

How a feature becomes required

  1. Anyone proposes a change as an issue or pull request.
  2. The change lands in the draft as optional (MAY or SHOULD), with schema changes and validator checks.
  3. A feature becomes required (MUST) only after two independent implementations pass the validator for it.
  4. Published schema versions never change. Breaking changes get a new version path.

Status and roadmap

  • Now: Draft 1.0, proposed, published 2026-09-23. Spec text, example objects and llms.txt are live.
  • Next: JSON Schemas at fixed URLs, the validator and badge, and three starters.
  • Proposed: a W3C Community Group. It is not formed yet. A Community Group can publish reports, but those are not W3C Recommendations.
  • Planned: provisional registration of the arv: URI scheme with IANA.
  • Not claimed: no outside implementations yet. The draft stays "proposed" until one passes the validator.

How to join

  • GitHub organization coming. Spec text, schemas, validator and starters in the open, with issues and pull requests.
  • W3C Community Group proposed. A free group where anyone with a W3C account can join, comment and back the work.
  • Email, open now: team@agentreadyvideo.org. Tell us what you publish or build and what you need from the spec.
