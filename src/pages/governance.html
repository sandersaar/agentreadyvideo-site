Governance

ARV is maintained by AgentCDN with open contributions. The spec is a draft, and it says "proposed" until an outside implementation passes the validator.

Maintainer

AgentCDN maintains the spec, the schemas and the validator. Contact: team@agentreadyvideo.org.

Status and roadmap

  • Now: Draft 1.0, proposed, published 2026-09-23 at /spec/1.0. Spec text, JSON Schemas at /schema/1.0/, example objects, the quick start and llms.txt are live. Every change is in the changelog.
  • Next: the validator and badge, and three starters.
  • Proposed: a W3C Community Group. It is not formed yet. A Community Group can publish reports, but those are not W3C Recommendations.
  • Submitted: provisional registration of the arv: URI scheme with IANA, requested 2026-09-24.
  • Filed: a schema.org guidance issue on Clip ids for video moments, schemaorg/schemaorg#4923.
  • Not claimed: no implementations yet (see implementations). The draft stays "proposed" until an outside one passes the validator.
